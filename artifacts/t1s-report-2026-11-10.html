SEO, Google Ranking & Affiliate Payout Report

What Google actually shows today, why the site sits at position ~50, which affiliate partners pay nothing right now, and the exact route to join each one.
Prepared 10 November 2026, 20:30 IST · Search data: Google Search Console, 28 days ending 9 November 2026 · Affiliate data: code audit of commit 89c28d8 plus LinkInR campaign sheet (166 approved campaigns) and Cuelinks account reads from 10 November

1. Summary, in five lines

  • Indexation is not the problem. Google returns "Submitted and indexed" for the pages checked by URL Inspection (/, /answers, /best/flights, /cabs/noida, /hotels/mumbai, /movies/noida). 194 of ~1,020 sitemap URLs showed at least one impression in 28 days.
  • Ranking is the problem. Average position 50.2, median query position 55.4, 2,447 impressions, 20 clicks, CTR 0.82%. At position 50 a sub-1% CTR is arithmetic, not a title failure.
  • Demand is already arriving in the money verticals. Loans drew 1,125 impressions and insurance 324 with zero clicks, because those pages rank far outside the visible results.
  • Affiliate routing is broad, but routing is not the same as being paid. 133 merchant domains are reachable; 84 have a network route, and only the ones with a joined campaign actually pay. 49 domains have no route at all.
  • One decision is waiting on you: today every finance exit goes to your GroMo advisor link, including issuers where LinkInR already pays a known amount (SBI Card ₹1,900, Axis up to ₹1,600, IndusInd ₹1,280). GroMo's own rate per approval is not visible in our click data, so this report lists both and gives the one-line switch.

The honest framing. Position 50 with 2,447 impressions means Google has decided the pages are relevant but not yet the best answer. CTR fixes (titles, schema) cannot move a page from 50 into the top 10. Only three levers can: query-phrase internal anchors, more real data per page, and third-party signals (backlinks, Play Store listing, Google Business Profile). None of those are claimed as done in this report.

3. Why the homepage gets the traffic, and the four fixes

27 homepage impressions produced 10 of the 20 clicks. The vertical and answer pages are indexed but receive no click pull, which usually means they are linked from navigation chrome (footer, menu) rather than from body copy that uses the query language people type.

FixWhat to changeWhere

6. The next 30 days, in order

  1. Week 1: internal anchor rewrite on the homepage and the six hub pages (query phrases, city links in body copy). This is the only change that can move position without new content.
  2. Week 1: decide the GroMo versus LinkInR question per issuer, then set GROMO_SKIP_DOMAINS for the losing side.
  3. Week 2: apply to every pending Cuelinks advertiser and to the LinkInR finance campaigns not yet joined. Both are dashboard work, no release cycle.
  4. Week 2: Play Store listing for the app, and a Google Business Profile. Both are third-party signals that no on-page work replaces.
  5. Week 3: 3 to 5 real data blocks per money page (price range, provider count, last updated) generated from rows already fetched, so loans and insurance stop being thin.
  6. Week 4: re-read GSC. Success criteria: loans and insurance clicks above zero, average position under 40, and at least 250 pages with impressions.
