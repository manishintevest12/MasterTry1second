3. Agla kadam

  1. Finance pehle (16 domains): BankBazaar/Paisabazaar/PolicyBazaar publisher programs — CPA sabse zyada.
  2. Travel/OTA (22 domains): Cuelinks/Admitad me campaign join, phir domain CUELINKS_EXTRA_MERCHANTS ya us network ke *_MERCHANTS me daal dein.
  3. Chain hotels (Marriott/Hilton/Accor/ITC/Taj): inke apne partner programs hain (Impact/partner portals) — direct onboarding better hai.
  4. Airlines: koi public affiliate nahi — in par sirf ads, isliye affiliate effort waste na karein.
  5. Har join ke baad wahi domain env me add karein — dobara code deploy nahi chahiye.
